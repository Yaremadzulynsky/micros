CONFIRM ACTION

CONNECT YOUR BOARD

Plug in. Run enroll.

Connect your board by USB. From your micros project, run:

micros enroll

Choose this server: . The CLI detects your board, installs missing tools, prepares its firmware and walks you through Wi-Fi.

The first firmware build can take several minutes. Once enrollment finishes, your board appears here. Open it and choose Deploy your first application to see the starter heartbeat in its logs.

New to micros? Install the CLI and create a project

Advanced: manual authorization

Authorize a known hardware identity and download credentials for manual provisioning. This does not install firmware or connect the board.
